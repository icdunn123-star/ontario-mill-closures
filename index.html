Method: Closure year is defined here as the first year after a recorded active (Y) year in which the facility is marked inactive (N) in the workbook's TREES_Matrix. This is an analytical proxy, not an independently confirmed physical mill shutdown date. Locations are town/city-centre approximations based on the workbook's FacilityLocation field; markers are not exact mill-site coordinates. Facilities with no town/city value in the workbook are omitted from the map (8 records) but retained in the source workbook. Basemap tiles © Esri, USGS, NOAA. Place-centre coordinates are approximate and should be treated as indicative.
